JOIFF Appendix D reference ranges for major fires: Low 9,000-18,000 L/min; Medium 18,000-27,000 L/min; High 27,000-54,000 L/min. This is a reference comparison, not a code-compliance verdict.
JOIFF field-test reminders: use the correct standpipe/adaptors and a flow/pressure test instrument that does not restrict flow; flush the hydrant/test setup before recording readings (the guideline notes typically 30 seconds); record static and residual pressure plus flow; turn hydrants off slowly; and coordinate tests with the local water authority. The guideline also states that gauges and flow meters should be calibrated at least every 12 months, or more frequently depending on use.

JOIFF defines static pressure as pressure with hydrants closed, residual pressure as pressure while flowing, and generally recommends retaining 1.4 bar (20 psi). For static pressure below 2.8 bar (40 psi), it says to rate at one-half static pressure. Appendix E uses the 0.54-power relationship to estimate flow at another residual pressure.
