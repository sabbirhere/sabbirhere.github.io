Basis & limitations

Rated current = kVA / (√3·kV). Through-fault current at the HT side for an LV bus fault = rated current / (Zt + Zsource) in per-unit on the transformer base (Dyn transformer assumed; Zt −7.5% for maximum and +7.5% for minimum). 51 pickup = multiple of rated current, rounded up to 0.05×In. 51 TMS is the smallest value giving (LV main clearing time + CTI) at the maximum through-fault. 50 = margin × maximum through-fault, also checked against inrush and the minimum HT bus fault. 51N = fraction of the maximum earth-fault current (residual CT floor 10% of CT primary). IEC 60255 IDMT curves: t = TMS·k/((I/Is)^α − 1). Not covered: 87T/REF differential, thermal (49), Buchholz/OTI/WTI trips, LV-side protection settings, motor starting, CT saturation study, arc flash. For a Dyn transformer, LV earth faults are not seen by HT earth-fault protection.
