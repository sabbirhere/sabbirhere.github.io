1. Insulation Resistance & PI

HT-Earth • LT-Earth • HT-LT
DETAILED FIELD PROCEDURE & WIRING
Isolation & Safety:
  • Disconnect all incoming HV and LV cables/busbars from transformer bushings. Apply working earths.
  • Clean and wipe down all bushing porcelains to prevent surface leakage current.
  • Test 1: HT Winding to Earth (HT → Earth)
    • Shorting: Using bare copper wire, short together all HT bushings (H1, H2, H3, H0 if applicable).
    • Shorting: Short together all LT bushings (X1, X2, X3, X0) and connect this shorted bundle directly to Main Tank Earth (Ground).
    • Instrument Connections: Connect LINE (High) Lead to the shorted HT bushings. Connect EARTH (Low) Lead to Tank Earth. Connect GUARD Lead to shorted LT bushings (or bushing guard rings) if present.
    • Execution: Apply 5000 V DC. Record R30, R60 (1 min), and R600 (10 min).
    • Discharge: Stop instrument and ground the HT shorted group for at least 4 minutes using a discharge rod before touching.
  • Test 2: LT Winding to Earth (LT → Earth)
    • Shorting: Keep LT bushings (X1, X2, X3, X0) shorted together. Un-ground them from tank earth.
    • Shorting: Short together all HT bushings (H1, H2, H3, H0) and connect the HT bundle to Main Tank Earth.
    • Instrument Connections: Connect LINE Lead to shorted LT bushings. Connect EARTH Lead to Tank Earth. Connect GUARD Lead to shorted HT bushings.
    • Execution: Apply 1000 V DC (or 2500 V DC per spec). Record R30, R60, and R600.
    • Discharge: Stop instrument and ground LT shorted group for at least 4 minutes using a discharge rod.
  • Test 3: HT Winding to LT Winding (HT → LT)
    • Shorting: Keep HT bushings (H1, H2, H3, H0) shorted together. Keep LT bushings (X1, X2, X3, X0) shorted together. Neither bundle is connected to tank earth.
    • Instrument Connections: Connect LINE Lead to shorted HT bushings. Connect EARTH Lead to shorted LT bushings. Connect GUARD Lead directly to Main Tank Earth.
    • Execution: Apply 5000 V DC. Record R30, R60, and R600.
    • Discharge: Stop instrument and ground BOTH HT and LT shorted groups for at least 4 minutes.

Important: Always verify zero voltage with an approved multimeter before removing or reconfiguring leads.

2. Transformer Turns Ratio (TTR)

Nameplate comparison
DETAILED FIELD PROCEDURE & WIRING
Preparation & Connections:
  • Remove all shorting wires from previous IR tests. Do NOT short terminals during TTR testing.
  • Ground the TTR test set chassis directly to Main Tank Earth.
  • HV Cable Connections (Primary Side):
    • Connect H1 (Red) lead to Transformer Bushing H1.
    • Connect H2 (Yellow) lead to Transformer Bushing H2.
    • Connect H3 (Blue) lead to Transformer Bushing H3.
    • Connect H0 (Black) lead to Transformer Bushing H0 (Neutral, if present).
  • LV Cable Connections (Secondary Side):
    • Connect X1 (Red) lead to Transformer Bushing X1.
    • Connect X2 (Yellow) lead to Transformer Bushing X2.
    • Connect X3 (Blue) lead to Transformer Bushing X3.
    • Connect X0 (Black) lead to Transformer Bushing X0 (Neutral, if present).
  • Instrument Setup: Select Vector Group on TTR instrument (e.g., Dyn11, Ynd11) and input nominal primary/secondary voltages.
  • Execution: Set On-Load Tap Changer (OLTC) / Off-Circuit Tap Changer (OCTC) to Tap 1. Inject low AC excitation voltage (e.g., 80 V or 100 V AC). Run measurement on all 3 phases.
  • Tap Sequence: Step through all tap positions (e.g., Tap 1 to Tap N). Record measured ratio, phase deviation, and percentage error for every phase on every tap.

Caution: Ensure high voltage leads are strictly connected to HV bushings and low voltage leads to LV bushings to prevent instrument damage and step-up hazards.

Use the approved test specification or manufacturer requirement.

3. Winding Resistance

Compare all available phases
DETAILED FIELD PROCEDURE & WIRING
Four-Wire (Kelvin) Setup:
  • Use a 4-wire DC micro-ohmmeter/winding resistance meter. Connect Current leads (C+, C-) and Potential leads (P+, P-) directly to bushing metal terminals.
  • HV Winding Measurement (Delta Connection Example: H1-H2, H2-H3, H3-H1):
    • Phase A (H1-H2): Connect C+ and P+ to Bushing H1. Connect C- and P- to Bushing H2.
    • Phase B (H2-H3): Connect C+ and P+ to Bushing H2. Connect C- and P- to Bushing H3.
    • Phase C (H3-H1): Connect C+ and P+ to Bushing H3. Connect C- and P- to Bushing H1.
  • LV Winding Measurement (Star/Wye Connection Example: X1-X0, X2-X0, X3-X0):
    • Connect C- and P- to Neutral Bushing X0.
    • Connect C+ and P+ sequentially to X1, X2, and X3.
  • Execution: Inject DC current (typically 1% to 10% of rated winding current; do not exceed 15%). Wait for core saturation until resistance reading stabilizes completely.
  • De-energization: Initiate instrument's AUTOMATIC DISCHARGE / DE-FLUX cycle. Wait for discharge indicator to show zero current before disconnecting!

Warning: High inductive energy stored in windings. Disconnecting live leads will cause severe electrical arcing!

Use the approved specification. The tool reports spread around the phase average.

4. Oil Breakdown Voltage (BDV)

IEC 60156 method
DETAILED FIELD PROCEDURE & SAMPLING
Sampling & Testing Procedure (per IEC 60156):
  • Drain at least 2–3 liters of oil from bottom sampling valve to flush line contaminants.
  • Collect fresh oil sample using a clean, dry glass bottle. Cap tightly immediately.
  • Set electrode gap to exactly 2.50 mm (±0.05 mm) using a precision gauge block.
  • Gently pour sample oil into test cell. Allow cell to sit undisturbed for 10 minutes for air bubbles to escape.
  • Run 6 consecutive breakdowns with automated magnetic stirring between shots.

Record: Sample ID, oil temperature, electrode gap, 6 breakdown values, and average kV.

5. Bushing / Winding Tan Delta

Temperature dependent
DETAILED FIELD PROCEDURE & WIRING
Power Factor / Tan δ Configurations:
  • Ensure transformer tank, core, and test-set ground leads are tied firmly to main substation earth grid.
  • C1 Main Bushing Test (GST-Guard Mode): Connect HV Output to Main Bushing Top Stud, MEASURE Lead to Bushing Test Tap, GUARD to Tank Earth. Apply 10 kV AC.
  • C2 Bushing Tap Test (UST Mode): Connect HV Output to Bushing Test Tap, MEASURE Lead to Main Bushing Top Stud. Apply 500 V – 2 kV AC.

Warning: Always ground bushing test taps before leaving unit. Operating with an open test tap destroys the bushing!

6. DGA — Acetylene

Diagnostic input
DETAILED FIELD PROCEDURE & SAMPLING
Dissolved Gas Analysis Sampling (per ASTM D3613 / IEC 60567):
  • Use a 50 mL or 100 mL gas-tight glass syringe with a 3-way stopcock.
  • Flush line, collect sample without pulling forcefully on plunger to prevent vacuum air bubbles.
  • Store syringe in dark transport box and send to laboratory for Gas Chromatography analysis.

7. SFRA / FRA Recording

Comparison required
DETAILED FIELD PROCEDURE & WIRING
Sweep Frequency Response Analysis Wiring Setup:
  • All ground leads MUST use shortest path high-frequency flat copper grounding straps screwed directly to bushing base flange.
  • Connect SOURCE + REFERENCE to Input Bushing. Connect MEASUREMENT to Output Bushing. Run frequency sweep (20 Hz to 2 MHz).
