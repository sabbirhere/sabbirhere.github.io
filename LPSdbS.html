Building

Plan length and width of the roof, and height from ground to the eave. These set the collection area, mesh and down-conductor count. Length along the LPS from the point under review down to the nearest equipotential bonding or earth point. Used for separation distance s. With the mesh method the roof conductors are the air termination, so rods are needed only at exposed corners and ends. Extra rods are optional.

Legacy risk screen (not code-compliance assessment)

Enter ground strike-point density NSG, which is higher than the older flash density NG. How exposed the building is to direct strikes (IEC 62305-2 Cd). C2–C5 give the tolerable frequency NC = 5.5×10⁻³ / (C2·C3·C4·C5). If ND > NC, LPS efficiency E = 1 − NC/ND selects the class. Materials, separation and earthing

Sets the minimum above-ground conductor sizes. Aluminium must not be buried or used in contact with soil/concrete. Solid round is a solid wire, not a pipe. Sizes below follow the form selected here. Used independently from the above-ground conductor material; use compatible, corrosion-controlled transitions. Insulating material between the LPS and metalwork at the separation point. Share of lightning current in the nearest down conductor. Use 0.44 only with an interconnected Type B ring. From a Wenner 4-pin test. Measure in the dry season; wet-monsoon readings are lower and not conservative. User-selected target for this preliminary sizing model; 10 Ω is only the starting value and is not a universal IEC or BNBC acceptance limit. Set the value required by the project and authority. Type B is preferred with several down conductors. Minimum 16 mm galvanised steel or 15 mm copper (IEC 62305-3 Table 7).
