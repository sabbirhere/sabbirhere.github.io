Automatic input check

ElectraLab tries every ON/OFF combination of the inputs used by your ladder and shows which outputs turn on. This quick check is a behavior preview—not a guess at what your machine is supposed to do. Use the test builder below for timer delays or repeated counter pulses.

        Automatic input check shows the ladder’s behavior for each input combination. It cannot tell whether that behavior matches your design until you set expectations in a specific test.

        Simulator health checks the underlying PLC engine, not your ladder program. These checks do not certify a real machine, a vendor PLC, or a safety circuit.
